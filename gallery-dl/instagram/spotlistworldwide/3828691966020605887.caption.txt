45 minutes from Paris 🪄🏰

Hidden in the forest outside the city, Abbaye des Vaux de Cernay feels like stepping into a real-life castle: soaring stone arches, candlelit vibes, cozy lounges, fireplaces… every corner looks straight out of a movie.

Bike rides through the woods, an unreal brunch, floating padel on the lake, a heated pool, and nights in a setting worthy of a film. The perfect spot for a surprise weekend, a winter escape… or simply to live your Harry Potter fantasy. ✨

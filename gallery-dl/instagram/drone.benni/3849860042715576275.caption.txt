🏰 Burg Taufers, South Tyrol, Italy

Burg Taufers is one of the most impressive medieval castles in South Tyrol. Built in the 13th century, it sits above the village of Sand in Taufers and offers breathtaking views of the surrounding Alps. With more than 60 rooms, historic halls, and original medieval details, the castle gives a fascinating glimpse into life during the Middle Ages.

These aerial shots were filmed with the DJI Air 3S, capturing the castle and its dramatic alpine setting from above.

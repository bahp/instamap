Tarasp Castle is a striking medieval fortress perched high on a rocky hill in the Lower Engadine region of Switzerland, overlooking the Inn Valley. Dating back to the 11th century, the castle was built to control important alpine routes and to assert authority over the surrounding territory. Its tall towers, thick stone walls, and elevated position give it a commanding presence, visible from miles away. The dramatic setting against snow-capped peaks and alpine meadows enhances its timeless and almost legendary character.

Carefully restored in the early 20th century, Tarasp Castle today combines medieval architecture with later historic elements. The interior features richly decorated rooms, including an ornate chapel and grand halls furnished with artworks and period details. Open to visitors through guided tours, the castle offers insight into both feudal life and later aristocratic tastes. Standing proudly above the valley, Tarasp Castle remains one of Switzerland’s most iconic landmarks, symbolizing power, resilience, and the enduring influence of history within a majestic mountain landscape.

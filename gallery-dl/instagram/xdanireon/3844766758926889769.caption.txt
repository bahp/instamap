Two Dark Dragonite Cards, but one is worth 100 times more than the other 👀 

This is the Dark Dragonite No Holo Error - and you may have one sitting in your old binder 

If you look at both card numbers, you'll notice that one is numbered 5/82 – which is the card number for the Holo Dark Dragonite, making this variant a rare error to find

The error exists in both Unlimited and 1st Edition prints, with the 1st edition variant being much harder to find 

To this day, only 19 PSA 10s have been graded for this error 💠

During my time at Woodbridge Card Show yesterday, I had the pleasure of meeting @oterodactyl, who hooked me up with some sweet custom cards! You guys already know how much I love the “stained glass”-style for cards, so picking this spooky piece out was a no-brainer! The Gengar line is arguably one of the most iconic evolutionary trios in all of Pokémon, with Gengar itself being one of the most famous ghosties. This trio looks absolutely-fantastic in this art style, and it makes me wish we got more official stained glass cards in the game! Check his page out!

Buried deep in the mountains of Akita prefecture lies Tsuru no yu, a traditional mountain onsen at the foot of Mt Nyuto. 

In winter, this area experiences incredible snowfall, creating one of the most magical scenes I've ever seen 🥹💕

Getting a reservation here is pretty hard, since both ryokan in this area are rather small with only a handful of rooms. You can also visit as a daytrip guest though (Mondays usually closed). 

❣️ Make sure to check their website before coming here so you don't make the trip in vain. Be sure to drive a 4wd with winter tires (mandatory) as a small mountain road covered in snow and ice only leads to this area. If you're not confident driving, there is a shuttle bus that runs a few times daily 🚌

🎨 Spotlight on Aya Kusube - The Storybook-Weird Side of Pokémon Art ✍️✨

Ever flip past a Pokémon card and go, “wait… this one feels different”? 👀
That hand-drawn, textured, slightly sketchy look that doesn’t feel like the usual polished anime style? That’s Aya Kusube’s signature.

Her cards have this classic illustration feel with expressive lines, unique shading, and compositions that look like they came straight out of a storybook 📖
Sometimes cozy, sometimes quirky, sometimes even a little eerie… but always memorable. These are the cards that stand out in a binder because they don’t look like everything else.

Not every Aya Kusube card is a flashy chase… but they age beautifully in collections. The kind you appreciate more the deeper you get into the hobby 🧠💭

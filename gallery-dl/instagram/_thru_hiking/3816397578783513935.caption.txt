Wolf Trail 🇵🇱🇩🇪🇨🇭🇦🇹🇸🇮🇮🇹 Europe’s newest thru-hiking trail | 3493 km ~ 2170 miles | 4-6 months | info 👇

The Wolf Trail is a new thru-hike that goes coast-to-coast from northern Poland to Trieste on the Adriatic Sea in Italy. It stitches together existing premier routes including the E9 (Coastal Path), E1, Nord–Süd Route, and the Via Alpina into one seamless odyssey. It crosses six countries through forests, countryside, mountains, and the Mediterranean. We’re very stoked to launch this new thru-hiking trail 🤩

If you don’t have the time to hike the full Wolf Trail, no worries, you can also very easily section-hike it 🙏

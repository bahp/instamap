If you need an easy outdoor setup for a sunny day, shadow tracing is the move. It’s such a fun way to get them outside and working on those fine motor skills while you grab a coffee (or join in!).

All you need is a roll of paper, some sunshine, and their favorite plastic figurines. It’s amazing to see how the “art” changes as the sun moves! 🖍️✨

Christmas dream destination in Europe 🎄

This European Christmas city feels straight out of a fairytale 😍 It’s like walking through a storybook! I kept stopping every few steps because every single corner looked like a postcard. 

Everywhere you look there are fairy lights and festive treats. I was basically running on waffles and hot chocolate the whole time and not mad about it 😆

The Bruges Christmas markets are so cute and at night the Winter Glow light trail lights up the city. Honestly, it just feels like Christmas here ✨

If you’re planning a festive trip in Europe, this place needs to be on your list 🎄

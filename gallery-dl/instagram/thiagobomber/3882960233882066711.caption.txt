📍Churburg Castel Coira • Sluderno Bolzano 

Construction began around 1260, at the initiative of the Prince-Bishop of Chur, Heinrich von Montfort. The core of the castle consisted of the tower, the surrounding wall, and the palace. It was built to contain the expansion of the Lords of Matsch.

The diocese of Chur succumbed to Meinhard II of Tyrol-Gorizia, who gave the castle to the Matsch family in 1297. In 1504, thanks to the marriage of the last Matsch heir to a Count of Trapp, the castle passed to the latter, who still own it today.

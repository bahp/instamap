With the new film adaptation of Wuthering Heights on the way, we had to stop by the little Yorkshire town that shaped the world of the iconic Brontë sisters ✨

Haworth feels like stepping straight into one of their novels. Cobbled streets, rolling moors, moody skies and that wild Yorkshire air, and suddenly it all makes sense how Charlotte Brontë, Emily Brontë and Anne Brontë dreamt up such intense, windswept stories right here! You can almost picture them walking these same paths, caught up in their own thoughts, before heading back to the parsonage, which you can still visit, to write their stories 🪶📖

📍 Haworth, West Yorkshire, England 
🚙 4+ hours

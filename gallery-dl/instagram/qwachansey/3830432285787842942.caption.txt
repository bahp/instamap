My master set of textured Japanese BW full arts (main set + promos) shinies, and gold item cards. This was a difficult binder to complete but I absolutely adore it and hope to do the same with XY Era one day.
Fun Fact: I was able to confirm directly with Kouki Saitou, who illustrated the ultra rare Meowth and Pikachu from BW1, that these artworks are NOT the Shiny version. It was extremely cool to meet him and have him sign these cards for me. I wonder why they switched to Shinies - maybe wanted to first introduce the new glitter texture and embossed style with “normal” popular Pokemon?
Second Fun Fact: There are three Japanese-exclusive cards in this binder (15th Anniversary Reshiram & Zekrom, 15th Anniversary Promo Pikachu) which were available at Pokemon Centers in Japan. There are also two Asia-exclusive unique artwork promos Rayquaza and Giratina ex. These were distributed as part of a lottery in Japan with 1,000 winners of each card. This lottery also extended to Korea with Korean versions of the promos.

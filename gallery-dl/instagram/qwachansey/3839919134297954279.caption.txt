All 151 Carddass Pokémon from the 1997 set in their original binder! I am missing the two checklist cards. These cards are believed to be illustrated by Ken Sugimori and are infamous for their surprisingly violent depictions of battle moves! They are slightly smaller than the typical card. 1996 & 1997 were really the wild west of Pokémon where artists had more liberty to illustrate as they liked and take creative licenses before stricter brand guidelines were put together.

🚄 I took the Coast Starlight @amtrak from Los Angeles to Seattle, it’s one of the most scenic train routes in the U.S.

The ride takes you through California’s coast, Oregon’s forests, and Washington’s mountains. 

It’s long, yes, but there’s something peaceful about letting time stretch out while the scenery keeps changing 🌊🌲

📍 Odai Sushi
🍣 A tiny family home turned into a sushi counter, where the chef serves what’s fresh that morning, piece by piece, in silence.
🎌 No sign outside, no menu. Just decades of mastery hidden in a quiet Kyoto residential street.
✅ 10 seats only. Walk-ins. Bring respect, it’s a residential area.

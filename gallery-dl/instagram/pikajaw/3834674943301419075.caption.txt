cosmic stars and the city lights 🌃🌟💗

i felt really inspired to create a pokemon binder page that blends in stars and neonwave vibes in one spread and these were the cards i was able to put together! i'm so happy with how the page looks🥹

some of the pokemon tcg cards that have stars and its elements are jirachi, cleffa wishing upon a star, the cutest quaxly chinese card, meowth with the lanterns and you can't forget minior whenever stars are invloved💫

as for the neon city lights, the underrated iron jugulis from the paradox pokemon - the neon lights while the pokemon is flying awayy hehe, and the team rocket ariana with murkrow is so iconic with the skycraper view, and let's not forget the coolest duraludon card with the awesome neon lights✨ so pretty!!

the tag team togepi, cleffa and igglybuff are a great mix with the rest of the cards along with the solrock, keldeo and helioptile aura lights🌌

what card is your favorite from the bunch? for me it would have to be between the colourful minior and the lantern meowth - that card really reminds me of tangled🥺💖
